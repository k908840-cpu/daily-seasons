# Daily Seasons — Google Play release preparation

## Store listing draft

**App name:** Daily Seasons

**Short description (under 80 characters):** Gentle Christian prayer, Scripture, and daily reflection.

**Full description:**

Make space for prayer throughout the Christian year with Daily Seasons, a calm companion for Scripture, reflection, and traditional Christian devotion.

- Read the World English Bible offline, search passages, and save notes and bookmarks.
- Explore original prayers and selected historic Christian prayers with source information.
- Follow guided prayer routines, Lectio Divina, Rosary, and seasonal reflections.
- Personalize your experience by Christian tradition, prayer interests, and preferred duration.
- Save private prayer intentions, journal entries, and progress on your device.
- Set optional daily reminders and export a personal backup.

No account required. No advertisements or analytics. Designed for quiet, intentional use.

**Important:** Lifetime purchases are not available in the current preview build. Do not advertise paid unlocks or submit a production paid listing until Google Play Billing is implemented, verified, and tested.
