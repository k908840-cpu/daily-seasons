# Daily Seasons — Privacy Policy (draft for publication)

**Effective date:** October 20, 2026

Daily Seasons is a Christian prayer and Bible reading application designed to work primarily offline. This policy describes the app as currently implemented. Review it against the final production build and any services enabled before publication.

## Information stored on your device

Daily Seasons stores your selected preferences, reading progress, bookmarks, Scripture notes and highlights, journal entries, prayer intentions, and devotional progress locally on your device. It does not require you to create an account.

## Data sharing and backup

Daily Seasons does not include advertising, analytics, or automatic cloud synchronization. You can choose to export your data using your device's share sheet. The backup is unencrypted text and may include sensitive personal reflections. If you choose a third-party app or cloud service to share or save it, that service's privacy practices apply. Daily Seasons does not automatically transmit backups.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders and grant permission, Daily Seasons schedules local notifications on your device. You can change notification permissions in Android settings.

## Scripture and external links

Scripture is packaged for offline reading. If you open external source or information links, your browser and the destination website may process information according to their own policies.

## Purchases

In the current preview, Google Play purchases are disabled. If billing is enabled in a future release, purchases will be processed by Google Play, subject to Google's payment and privacy policies. This policy will be updated to accurately describe the final implementation.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android device backup settings. You can export a copy before deleting the app. Data sent through third-party sharing tools is not deleted by uninstalling Daily Seasons.

## Children

Daily Seasons is not designed to collect children's personal information. The app has no user accounts or in-app advertising.
