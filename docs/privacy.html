Privacy Policy for One Faith: Bible & Prayer

Effective date: October 21, 2026

One Faith: Bible & Prayer (“One Faith,” “we,” or “the app”) is a Bible reading, prayer, and devotional app. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the Android app.

Information you enter

You may create journal entries, prayer intentions, notes, bookmarks, and preferences. These are stored locally on your device to provide the app’s features. The app does not require an account and does not provide automatic cloud synchronization of this content.

How information is used

Locally stored information is used to display and maintain your entries, reading progress, saved content, and preferences. We do not use your journal or prayer content for advertising or profiling, and we do not operate a server that receives this content as part of the app’s normal operation.

Backups and sharing

If you choose to export a backup, the app creates a JSON text backup that can include personal journal entries, prayers, and other saved information. You choose whether and where to share or store this file using your device’s sharing options. Exported backups are not encrypted by the app. Anyone with access to the exported file may be able to read its contents. Handling of files by an external app or storage provider is governed by that provider’s policies. Importing a backup reads the file you select to restore information on your device.

Notifications and device permissions

With your permission, the app can schedule prayer or devotional reminders using Android notifications. You can change notification permission in your device settings. The app may use on-device features such as audio playback and text-to-speech to provide its content.

Third-party services and platform data

One Faith is built using Expo and React Native and runs on Android. Android, Google Play, and any external apps you deliberately use to share a backup may process technical or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. We do not include advertising or in-app analytics services as part of the app’s intended functionality. This statement does not override any data handling performed independently by your device operating system or app marketplace.

Data retention and deletion

Your app entries and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app, subject to any copies you have exported or backups maintained by your device or other services. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete files you previously exported elsewhere.

Children's privacy

One Faith does not require users to submit personal information to us and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of device sharing and backup features.

Security

We aim to minimize collection of personal information by keeping devotional content on your device. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device and treat exported backup files as sensitive.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revised policy will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
